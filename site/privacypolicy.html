1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pruda GbR ("we", "us", "our") processes personal data in connection with Discord bots and Discord applications operated by us (collectively, the "Bots" or "Services").

Our Bots provide various functions within Discord servers, including, depending on the individual Bot and its configuration, moderation, administration, support, ticket systems, commands, automated actions, logging, role management, server configuration and similar Discord-related functionality.

We are committed to processing personal data only to the extent necessary for providing, securing, maintaining and administering the Services.

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable data protection law, in particular Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and, where applicable, the German Federal Data Protection Act ("BDSG").

Where mandatory data protection laws of another jurisdiction apply to a particular user, those laws may apply in addition to this Privacy Policy.

  • spam and abuse prevention;
  • deletion of messages;
  • warnings;

  • moderation logs;

4. Discord as a Separate Service Provider

Our Bots operate through the Discord platform and the Discord API.

Discord is operated independently by Discord Inc. and processes personal data for its own purposes under its own terms and privacy policies.

Our Bots may receive certain information from Discord through the Discord API where this is technically required for the respective Bot functionality.

We do not control how Discord itself processes information through its platform.

Users should therefore also review Discord's own Privacy Policy and applicable Terms of Service.

The fact that certain information can technically be accessed through the Discord API does not mean that we permanently store such information.

5. Categories of Data We May Process

Depending on the functionality used, our Bots may process certain information provided through Discord.

This may include the following categories.

5.1 Discord User Information

We may process:

  • Discord User ID;
  • Discord username;
  • Discord display name;
  • Discord server nickname;
  • Discord avatar or avatar identifier;
  • Discord roles;
  • Discord permissions;
  • Discord server memberships;
  • account-related information made available through the Discord API; and
  • other basic Discord account information technically required for a particular Bot function.

A Discord User ID is a persistent identifier and may therefore constitute personal data even if we do not know the real-world identity of the user.

We generally do not know who a Discord user is outside Discord solely on the basis of this information.

6. Discord Server Information

The Bots may also process information relating to Discord servers, including:

  • Discord Server/Guild ID;
  • Discord Channel IDs;
  • Discord Category IDs;
  • Discord Role IDs;
  • Discord Message IDs;
  • Discord Interaction IDs;
  • server settings;
  • Bot configuration;
  • role configuration;
  • channel configuration;
  • permission configuration;
  • timestamps; and
  • other identifiers required for operating the Bot.

Such information may constitute personal data where it can be associated with a particular Discord user.

7. Messages and Message Content

Certain Bot functions require technical access to Discord messages or message content.

This particularly applies to functions such as:

  • automated moderation;
  • spam detection;
  • word or content filtering;
  • command recognition;
  • support functions;
  • ticket functions;
  • message deletion;
  • automatic responses;
  • rule enforcement; and
  • security or abuse detection.

For example, a moderation Bot may need to analyse a message in order to determine whether it violates a configured moderation rule.

No permanent storage of ordinary Discord messages

As a general rule, we do not permanently store the content of ordinary Discord messages in our own database.

Message content may be processed temporarily or in real time where this is technically necessary to perform a Bot function.

For example, a message may be analysed in order to:

  • execute a command;
  • identify spam;
  • identify prohibited content;
  • trigger an automated response; or
  • determine whether the message should be deleted.

Unless a specific function expressly requires otherwise, the message content is not permanently copied into our database.

The deletion of a message by a Bot does not require us to permanently store the content of that message beforehand.

Discord itself may continue to process or retain messages independently under Discord's own policies.

8. Tickets and Support Functions

Some Bots may provide ticket or support functionality within Discord.

Where a user opens or participates in a ticket, the Bot may process information such as:

  • Discord User ID;
  • Discord Server ID;
  • Discord Channel ID;
  • ticket ID;
  • ticket creator;
  • assigned support member;
  • ticket status;
  • category;
  • creation timestamp;
  • closing timestamp; and
  • other metadata required to operate the ticket.

Messages exchanged within a Discord ticket may technically be accessed by the Bot where necessary to provide the ticket functionality.

Unless expressly stated otherwise for a specific function, we do not permanently copy the complete content of ticket conversations into a separate external database.

Ticket messages may remain stored within Discord itself for as long as the relevant Discord channel or messages continue to exist.

9. Commands and Discord Interactions

When users interact with a Bot through commands, slash commands, buttons, forms, menus or similar Discord features, we may process information such as:

  • Discord User ID;
  • Discord Server ID;
  • Discord Channel ID;
  • Interaction ID;
  • command name;
  • command parameters;
  • selected options;
  • timestamp; and
  • information required to return the requested result.

Such information is processed primarily to execute the requested command or interaction.

Limited metadata may additionally be retained for a maximum of 14 days where necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting or technical operation (see Section 19).

10. Moderation Data

Where our Bots provide moderation functionality, certain moderation-related records may be stored.

Depending on the configuration of the respective Discord server, this may include:

  • Discord User ID;
  • Discord Server ID;
  • Discord Moderator User ID;
  • type of moderation action;
  • moderation reason;
  • warning count;
  • moderation case ID;
  • timestamp;
  • duration of a timeout or similar action;
  • Discord Channel ID;
  • Discord Message ID; and
  • other information required to document or administer the moderation action.

Moderation records enable server administrators and moderators to consistently enforce server rules and investigate recent moderation actions. They are stored for a maximum of 14 days (see Section 19).

As a general rule, complete message content is not permanently stored solely because a moderation action occurred.

11. Log and Technical Data

For technical operation, security and troubleshooting purposes, our systems may process limited log information.

This may include:

  • timestamps;
  • Discord User IDs;
  • Discord Server IDs;
  • Discord Channel IDs;
  • command identifiers;
  • interaction identifiers;
  • error information;
  • error codes;
  • Bot events;
  • failed commands;
  • security events;
  • administrative actions; and
  • other technical information necessary for troubleshooting or protecting the Services.

Logs are stored for a maximum of 14 days (see Section 19) and are not intended to create behavioural profiles of users.

17. Automated Moderation

Certain Bots may automatically analyse messages or Discord activity in order to identify possible violations of configured server rules.

Automated moderation may, depending on server configuration:

  • delete messages;
  • issue warnings;
  • detect spam;
  • detect prohibited terms;
  • detect excessive mentions;
  • detect prohibited links;
  • apply temporary restrictions;
  • notify moderators;
  • assign or remove roles; or
  • flag activity for manual review.

Such systems operate according to rules and configurations determined for the relevant Discord server.

These systems are generally intended to support ordinary Discord community moderation and are not intended to make decisions producing legal effects concerning users.

Where required by applicable law, affected users may contact the relevant server moderation team or us to request review of an automated action.

18. Data Minimisation

We seek to process only information necessary to provide the respective Bot functionality.

The availability of information through the Discord API does not mean that we automatically store all information accessible through that API.

Where permanent storage is unnecessary, information may only be processed temporarily.

We do not intentionally create comprehensive user profiles based on Discord activity.

19. Storage Periods

Personal data processed by the Bots is stored for a maximum of 14 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law (see "Legal retention" below).

Data is deleted automatically once the 14-day period has expired.

In detail:

Message content

Ordinary Discord message content is not stored in our database. It is only processed in real time, in memory, where this is necessary to execute a command or a moderation, filtering or ticket function.

Command and interaction data

Command and interaction data (e.g. User ID, Server ID, command name, timestamp) is stored for a maximum of 14 days and then automatically deleted.

Moderation records

Moderation records (e.g. User ID, moderator ID, type of action, reason, timestamp) are stored for a maximum of 14 days and then automatically deleted.

Ticket metadata

Ticket-related metadata is stored for a maximum of 14 days and then automatically deleted.

Log and technical data

Log data is stored for a maximum of 14 days and then automatically deleted.

Bot configuration

Server IDs, role IDs, channel IDs and similar server configuration settings are stored only for as long as the Bot is installed on the relevant Discord server, as they are required for the Bot to function. Such configuration data does not contain personal data of individual users. When a Bot is removed from a Discord server, the associated configuration data is deleted within 14 days.

Backups

Backups are subject to the same maximum retention period of 14 days and are overwritten or deleted accordingly.

Legal retention

Information may only be retained for longer than 14 days where this is required by applicable law or is strictly necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims in a specific individual case. Such information is restricted from any other use and deleted as soon as the reason for retention no longer applies.

20. Removal of a Bot

If a Bot is removed from a Discord server, data exclusively required for the operation of that Bot on that server will no longer be required for normal operation.

Such data is deleted within 14 days after the Bot has been removed, unless a longer retention period is required by law or is strictly necessary for pending legal claims (see Section 19).

In addition, we will promptly delete data obtained through the Discord API where:

  • retaining it is no longer necessary for the stated functionality of the respective Bot;
  • we stop operating the respective Bot;
  • Discord requests that we delete it;
  • the affected user requests that we delete it; or
  • deletion is required by applicable law,

unless we are required by applicable law to retain it.
